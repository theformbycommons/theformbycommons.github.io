The Formby Commons

Streets & spaces that work better for everyone

The Formby Commons is a new grassroots community movement dedicated to making our streets and spaces more inclusive, safer, and enjoyable for all Formby residents — walkers, cyclists, drivers, children, elderly people, and families.

Mission Statement

The Formby Commons is a grassroots community movement dedicated to making our public streets and spaces more inclusive and enjoyable for everyone who lives, works, and learns in Formby. We recognise that most of us are drivers as well as pedestrians, and many of us walk or cycle when we can. Because most of us swap between driving, walking, and cycling depending on the day, we do not view transport as a competition between different road users. Our goal is to create safer, calmer, and more connected streets that work better for children walking or cycling to school, families, older residents, and all who want to get around easily on our flat, cycle-friendly terrain. We support practical improvements that reduce unnecessary short car trips while respecting the needs of those who drive.

By working together with residents, schools, and the council, we aim to encourage more independent travel to our many primary and secondary schools, ease school-run congestion, and build a stronger sense of community where people can pause, chat, and connect. We believe less reliance on cars for short journeys improves physical and mental health, supports local businesses, and creates safer spaces for all ages. This is not about drivers versus others, and not about party-politics. It is about practical choices that benefit and strengthen the whole community.

We are inspired by national ambitions for active travel and Sefton’s own plans, laid out in the ambitious Sefton Local Cycling and Walking Infrastructure Plan. Our movement will support evidence-based changes, foster collaboration between our two high schools and primary schools on a Formby Pupil Independent Travel Plan, and celebrate the many ways people already move around our town.

Our Goals

  • Create safer, more attractive routes for walking and cycling, especially around schools, public spaces, business hubs, and residential areas.
  • Particularly focus on protecting our most vulnerable road users, including children, pupils, the elderly, and those with reduced mobility.
  • Establish a collaborative Formby Pupil Independent Travel Plan uniting our 2 high schools and 6 primary schools.
  • Organise community events, school challenges, and practical advocacy.
  • Promote the benefits of active travel while remaining fully inclusive of drivers.

The Proven Benefits of Active Travel

Transitioning short, everyday journeys away from cars is heavily supported by public health and urban planning research. Academics globally have demonstrated that shifting to active transportation brings profound systemic benefits:

  • Physical Health and Longevity: Systematic reviews confirm that regular active travel significantly decreases all-cause mortality, reducing the long-term risk of cardiovascular diseases, type 2 diabetes, and metabolic disorders by integrating physical movement directly into daily routines (Saunders et al., 2013).
  • Mental Well-being and Mental Health: Swapping short car commutes for walking or cycling has been linked to lower levels of stress, reduced symptoms of anxiety and depression, and higher levels of daily life satisfaction (Mueller et al., 2015).
  • Community Cohesion: When traffic volumes and speeds decrease on residential roads, local street life increases. Quieter, safer streets allow residents to use public spaces for social interaction, fostering a sense of community belonging and neighborhood safety (Fitch-Polse & Agarwal, 2025).
