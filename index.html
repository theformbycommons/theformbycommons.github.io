The Formby Commons

Making our public streets and spaces more inclusive, safe, and welcoming for everyone.

About the Group

The Formby Commons is an informal community initiative started by local residents who care about our streets, public places, and shared environments.

We believe that public spaces work best when they are designed around people—whether you are walking to the shops, cycling to school, using a wheelchair, pushing a pram, driving, or catching public transport.

What We Focus On

  • Safer streets: Encouraging road designs and connections that protect pedestrians, cyclists, and vulnerable road users.
  • Accessibility for all: Ensuring our footpaths, village center, and public stops are easy to navigate for all generations and abilities.
  • Community connectivity: Linking our residential streets, village center, beach, and pinewoods cleanly and safely.
  • Local voice: Providing a calm, constructive platform for residents to share ideas and work collaboratively on practical local improvements.

Keeping in Touch

We hold quiet, periodic updates and occasional local gatherings to share thoughts on local spaces. There are no membership forms or commitments.
