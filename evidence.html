Evidence-Based Community Planning

Effective, positive community advocacy relies on clear data, direct local observations, and constructive dialogue. Rather than relying on guesswork, we collect and share insights about how people move around Formby.

  • Promote the benefits of active travel while remaining fully inclusive of drivers.

The Proven Benefits of Active Travel

Transitioning short, everyday journeys away from cars is heavily supported by public health and urban planning research. Academics globally have demonstrated that shifting to active transportation brings profound systemic benefits:

  • Physical Fitness & Active Commuting to School: Systematic reviews demonstrate that active school transport is directly associated with higher daily physical activity levels, lower body fat percentage, and improved cardiovascular fitness in young people (Larouche et al., 2014).
  • Promoting School Interventions: Targeted community and school-level active travel interventions consistently succeed in increasing walking and cycling rates among pupils, establishing health routines early in life (Chillón et al., 2011; Pang et al., 2017).
  • Community Benefits & Cycling Facilitators: Comprehensive reviews highlight that addressing safety perceptions, junction design, and traffic speeds are key facilitators for enabling widespread cycling across diverse population groups (Logan et al., 2023).
