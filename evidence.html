Evidence-Based Community Planning

Effective, positive community advocacy relies on clear data, direct local observations, and constructive dialogue. Rather than relying on guesswork, we collect and share insights about how people move around Formby.

Local active travel planning isn't just about cycling or walking in isolation; it is about creating street networks where multi-generational health, accessibility, and road safety work together. Designing streets around Vision Zero principles ensures that no severe injuries or fatalities on our local roads are accepted as inevitable.

Local Safety & Survey Data

In understanding resident experiences across key routes—such as school corridors, village center crossings, and connections to train stations—we gather empirical evidence to highlight where practical infrastructure improvements yield the biggest benefits.
